<p class="lead text-lg font-serif italic text-[#57534e] mb-6">Too often, tarot is reduced to theatrical fortune-telling—a passive attempt to spy on the future. But in my practice, the cards are held as a contemplative mirror: a rich visual language of archetypes that speaks directly to the subconscious mind.</p>

<p class="mb-4">Rooted in Carl Jung's concepts of synchronicity and active imagination, a thoughtful tarot reading does not proclaim that your fate is sealed in stone. Instead, it freezes a snapshot of your current psychological and energetic field, clarifying:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 mb-6 list-disc list-inside text-sm">
  <li>What unconscious desires or fears are secretly steering the vehicle?</li>
  <li>Where are you withholding your authentic voice in partnerships?</li>
  <li>What latent resources or creative instincts are waiting to be reclaimed?</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="font-serif text-2xl sm:text-3xl text-[#1c1917] mt-8 mb-4">A Sovereign Approach to the Cards</h2>
<p class="mb-4">You always retain free will. When difficult cards appear—such as The Tower or Ten of Swords—they are never causes for alarm. They are gracious warnings of where an unsustainable structure is ready to fall, liberating space for authentic rebuilding.</p>
