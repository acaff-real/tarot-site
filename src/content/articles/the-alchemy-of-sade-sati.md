<p class="lead text-lg font-serif italic text-[#57534e] mb-6">In popular astrology, few astrological passages evoke as much anxiety as <em>Sade Sati</em>—the approximate seven-and-a-half-year period when Shani (Saturn) transits the 12th, 1st, and 2nd houses from your natal Moon (Chandra).</p>

<p class="mb-4">Centuries of superstitious folklore have painted Saturn as a punitive force demanding appeasement through rituals. Yet in traditional Parashari Jyotish, Saturn represents <em>Vairagya</em> (sacred detachment), <em>Tapasya</em> (discipline), and uncompromised truth. Saturn is the cosmic auditor: he does not take anything that was genuinely yours to keep; he merely dismantles the illusions, ego dependencies, and fragile structures you outgrew years ago.</p>

<h2 class="font-serif text-2xl sm:text-3xl text-[#1c1917] mt-8 mb-4">The Three Phases of Shani's Transit</h2>
<p class="mb-4">Sade Sati unfolds in three distinct movements of approximately 2.5 years each:</p>
<ul class="space-y-3 mb-6 list-disc list-inside text-sm">
  <li><strong>First Phase (Transit through 12th from Moon):</strong> Mental unrest, financial recalibration, and feelings of isolation. This phase pulls attention inward, asking you to shed unnecessary commitments and resolve subconscious karmic debts.</li>
  <li><strong>Second Phase (Transit directly over Natal Moon):</strong> The core crucible. Saturn directly presses against your emotional sanctuary. It demands radical emotional maturity, psychological self-reliance, and physical groundedness.</li>
  <li><strong>Third Phase (Transit through 2nd from Moon):</strong> Stabilization of family dynamics, finances, and speech. The lessons learned during the peak phase now crystallize into enduring foundation and self-worth.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote class="p-6 my-8 rounded-lg bg-[#faf8f5] border-l-2 border-[#a37a4c] font-serif text-xl italic text-[#1c1917]">"Saturn does not hurry, and he does not bargain. When you surrender resistance and commit to quiet craftsmanship, Saturn grants what no other planet can: unshakeable inner sovereignty."</blockquote>

<h2 class="font-serif text-2xl sm:text-3xl text-[#1c1917] mt-8 mb-4">Constructive Alignment During Sade Sati</h2>
<p class="mb-4">Rather than adopting a defensive posture, approach this window as an intentional apprenticeship with life. Simplify your routines, honor commitments, practice generosity toward those less privileged (a direct remedy for Saturn), and welcome the quiet clarity that only disciplined simplicity can bring.</p>
