<p class="lead text-lg font-serif italic text-[#57534e] mb-6">In ancient Vedic science, sound is held as the primordial fabric of creation (<em>Nada Brahma</em>). Every syllable uttered generates a specific acoustic frequency that interacts with the nervous system and the energetic field.</p>

<p class="mb-4">Chaldean numerology, preserved for millennia across ancient Mesopotamia and integrated deeply into Indian astro-numerology, assigns numerical values to letters based on their sound vibration rather than their alphabetical sequence.</p>

<h2 class="font-serif text-2xl sm:text-3xl text-[#1c1917] mt-8 mb-4">Single Digits vs. Compound Numbers</h2>
<p class="mb-4">While the single root number (1 through 9) reveals your general archetype and planetary ruler, the compound number (the unreduced total, such as 19, 24, 27, or 37) reveals the hidden currents, trials, and blessings encoded within your name.</p>
<p class="mb-4">For example, while both 19 and 28 reduce to root number 1, compound 19 is known as 'The Prince of Heaven'—radiating joy, honor, and clear victory. Compound 28, conversely, indicates immense capability that must constantly navigate external competition and requires disciplined foresight.</p>
