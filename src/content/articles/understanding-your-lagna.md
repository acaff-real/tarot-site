<p class="lead text-lg font-serif italic text-[#57534e] mb-6">If you have only ever known yourself through a generic newspaper horoscope, you have likely encountered only a fraction of your celestial architecture. In Vedic astrology (Jyotish), the focal point of the entire birth chart is the <strong>Lagna</strong>, or Ascendant.</p>

<p class="mb-4">The Lagna is the exact degree of the eastern horizon rising at the precise moment you took your first breath. Because the earth rotates through all 12 signs in 24 hours, the Lagna shifts every two hours. It is intensely personal, capturing the specific doorway through which your consciousness entered physical form.</p>

<h2 class="font-serif text-2xl sm:text-3xl text-[#1c1917] mt-8 mb-4">The Three Pillars of Your Chart</h2>
<p class="mb-4">Vedic astrology harmonizes three primary coordinates to understand the complete individual:</p>
<ol class="space-y-3 mb-6 list-decimal list-inside text-sm">
  <li><strong>The Lagna (Ascendant):</strong> Your physical embodiment, vitality, worldview, and how you meet the world. It dictates the house cusps of all other eleven houses in your chart.</li>
  <li><strong>Chandra Lagna (Moon Sign):</strong> The psychological mind (<em>Manas</em>), emotional equilibrium, and your perceptual lens. In India, your Janma Rashi is considered the primary anchor of daily experience.</li>
  <li><strong>Surya Lagna (Sun Sign):</strong> The soul's core vitality (<em>Atman</em>), leadership capacity, and relationship to dharma and authority.</li>
</ol>

<p class="mb-4">When you calculate your true sidereal Lagna using the Lahiri Ayanamsha, you often discover that your rising sign shifts from its tropical Western position. For many clients, this recalculation feels like a homecoming—a sudden, clear explanation for why certain life patterns consistently repeat.</p>
